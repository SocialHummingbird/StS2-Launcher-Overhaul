## What Changed

- Publishes the bounded v6 shader warmup path for Android.
- First-run shader warmup now has a 90-second budget instead of repeatedly forcing weak devices through an unbounded precompile pass.
- `last_shader_warmup_status.txt` now distinguishes full `completed` warmup from `completed-partial`, where startup continues after the budget with degraded shader-cache coverage.
- Shader warmup diagnostics now record useful device details for minimum-spec triage, including model, Android/API version, processor count, low-RAM status, total memory, and Vulkan feature signals.
- Preserves the public-beta runtime-pack hardening, FMOD/audio bridge work, Steam auth-failure reporting, and current Workshop/mod support fixes from the previous releases.

## Known Caveats

- This is still prerelease/tester software, not final release-candidate signoff.
- ARM64 Android phones are the validated target.
- The current high-end validation device completed full warmup in about 40 seconds; weaker-device reports are still needed before publishing a precise RAM/GPU minimum.
- A `completed-partial` shader marker means the launcher hit its warmup budget and continued with degraded shader-cache coverage. That is useful compatibility evidence, not full device signoff.
- Shader scanner log noise can still appear while warmup completes and the game reaches the menu.
- Controller input and SavesMerger real-save compatibility still need broader device/user validation.
- No game files/assets are bundled. Steam ownership is required.
- Do not upload modded saves to Steam Cloud unless you know exactly what you are doing.
