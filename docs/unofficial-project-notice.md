# Unofficial Project Notice

StS2 Launcher is an unofficial Android launcher for people who already own Slay the Spire 2 on Steam.

The APK does not include Slay the Spire 2 game files, art, music, saves, or Steam Workshop content. After you sign in, the launcher downloads your copy from Steam and prepares it for ARM64 Android.

A Steam account that owns Slay the Spire 2 is required.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or supported by Mega Crit Games, Steam, or Valve. Slay the Spire 2 is developed and published by Mega Crit Games.

The StS2 Launcher name and startup animation identify this community tool, not an official mobile version of the game.

Android support is experimental. Some devices may have startup, graphics, audio or compatibility problems. Each release records what was tested and what remains unverified.

When sharing the project, describe it as:
